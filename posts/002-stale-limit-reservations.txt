We ran into this while investigating a suspicious metric in one of our payment services. The root cause was in how the service handled 𝗹𝗶𝗺𝗶𝘁 𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗲𝗿𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀 𝗱𝘂𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗮𝘆𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗹𝗶𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘆𝗰𝗹𝗲. Before executing a transaction, the Spring Boot service reserves part of the user’s limit to prevent concurrent overspending. The result looked like this: a user attempts a payment, the operation fails for some unrelated reason, the user retries a few seconds later, and the system immediately rejects the request because the limit still appears consumed.
