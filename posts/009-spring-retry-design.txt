That moment when external service fails once… and your whole flow breaks 😅

I used to handle retries “manually” — try/catch, counters, delays… messy and hard to maintain.
Then I started using 𝐒𝐩𝐫𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐑𝐞𝐭𝐫𝐲, and it changed how I design integrations.

In one of the payment flows, we had unstable external APIs. Not fully down — just random timeouts and occasional 5xx. Enough to hurt conversion and reliability.

Instead of overengineering, we introduced a simple approach:
→ 𝐑𝐞𝐭𝐫𝐲𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞 on critical calls
→ configurable attempts and backoff from properties
→ 𝐑𝐞𝐜𝐨𝐯𝐞𝐫 methods for fallback logic
→ proper logging to understand retry behavior

What I like most — it keeps business logic clean. No need to pollute services with retry loops.
But there was one important lesson: retries are not free.
If your operation is not idempotent — you’re just multiplying problems.

Now I treat retries as part of system design:
when to retry, how many times, and what happens if it still fails.
